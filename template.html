03 / Common questions

A few things to know.

What can we work on together?

Websites, landing pages, practical automation, connected customer workflows, and brand design. We confirm the exact scope before a project begins.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the scope, content readiness, and integrations. A schedule is discussed after we understand the requirements.

What assets do I need to provide?

Your logo, brand guidelines if available, existing content, and relevant project screenshots are a useful start. We identify any additional assets during planning.

Can you improve an existing website or connect my tools?

We first review your current website and tools. Possible changes and integrations depend on their access, capabilities, and the agreed scope.

Is ongoing support available?

Maintenance and ongoing support can be discussed as part of the project scope.

Does this demo send consultation requests?

No. You can test the form and review validation feedback, but the information stays in the current page and is not sent or stored.
